# telivi

> **తెలివి** — Telugu for intelligence.

An open model, trained on phones and browsers people lend, owned by the people who train it. [Apache 2.0](LICENSE).

**Status:** project definition. The product is [docs/PRD.md](docs/PRD.md). There is no training code yet.

Anyone can lend compute from a phone or a browser. The people who lend that compute hold a stake in the model. Each weight names the person who authored it, and keeps a chain of everyone who updated it.

The training and stake rules are public: [docs/RULES.md](docs/RULES.md).
